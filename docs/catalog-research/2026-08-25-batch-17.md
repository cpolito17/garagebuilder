- Scope: 25 new US-market vehicle generations.

## Model limitations

- The high-price collector rows (F50 and SVJ) are not liquid retail markets; their price curves are intentionally broad and should not be read as appraisals.
- The current Maverick, GX 550, Raptor, Sequoia, RS6, and Artura rows use clean, better-equipped examples as the price anchor, while the current MSRP field remains the new-car ceiling anchor.
- The Outback BT row uses the turbo XT specification because the non-turbo and turbo versions differ materially in towing and market price; the catalog keeps the generation together and explains the trim choice in `whatToLookFor`.
