# Catalog research log — batch 16

- Repository: `cpolito17/garagebuilder`
- Research date: 2026-08-24
- Scope: 25 new vehicle generations for the US catalog.
- Price convention: `pricing.base` is a clean-example asking-price estimate at
  `pricing.baselineMiles`, in USD. It is not original MSRP.
- Evidence convention: links below were retrieved on 2026-08-24. Specifications
  use manufacturer, press-kit, or established reference pages where available.
  Market calibration uses current US listing and auction-market pages.
- Limitation: the runtime schema stores catalog-level provenance rather than a
  source URL on each record, so this log is the retained per-batch evidence.
  Collector markets are explicitly marked where fewer than three
  condition-matched asks were available; no precision is implied.

## Known data limitations

- Asking-price pages move continuously and mix trim, mileage, seller, and
  condition; the selected base is a rounded editorial midpoint/clean-example
  estimate, not a live valuation.
- LFA, Carrera GT, and 918 Spyder prices are dominated by provenance and
  condition. Their low decay values are a coarse compromise for the app's
  mileage-only price curve.
- The batch uses representative powertrains for broad generation records.
  Trim-specific premiums remain in prose or are deliberately excluded from
  the generation-wide base.
