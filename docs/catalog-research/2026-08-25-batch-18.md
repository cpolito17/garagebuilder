## Model limitations

- The 765LT, F1, Enzo, and LaFerrari rows are thin collector markets. Auction CMB and recorded sales are retained as evidence, but mileage curves are only rough search-model inputs.
- The Equinox EV and Crown Signia are new-to-market vehicles with limited used samples; the selected bases lean on current asks plus MSRP, not on a mature depreciation history.
- The Land Cruiser J250 uses a loaded-trim MSRP anchor so its clean low-mile price is not forced below the requested $60,000 tier; this is stated rather than hidden in the curve.
